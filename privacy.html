Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026. This policy explains what personal data Classic Auto collects through this website and Anita, our chat assistant, why we collect it, and the rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

When you browse stock, use the EMI calculator or the Studio, we don't collect any personal data — those run entirely in your browser. We only collect personal data when you give it to us directly, through the "Schedule a visit" form, the "Sell Your Car" form, or a conversation with Anita: your name, phone number, the car you're interested in, your stated budget, and your preferred visit date/time.

Why we collect it (purpose limitation)

We use this data only to respond to your enquiry — to confirm a showroom visit, follow up on a sale/exchange request, or connect you with the right salesperson for your budget. We do not use it for anything you haven't asked for, and we don't sell it to anyone.

How it's collected and stored

Every form on this site, and Anita's chat hand-off, submit to the same place: either a WhatsApp message you send us yourself (nothing leaves your device until you tap send), or — once we switch on our backend — a direct submission to Classic Auto's own systems. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or sell data to data brokers.

Your rights under the DPDP Act

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; ask us to correct or update it; ask us to erase it once it's no longer needed for the purpose you gave it to us; withdraw consent at any time (as easily as you gave it); and nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you're unable to. To exercise any of these, message us on WhatsApp at +91 95889 90000 or call the showroom — we'll act on a verified request within a reasonable time.

Retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to complete the transaction it relates to (a visit, a sale, a purchase) plus a reasonable period for follow-up and our legal/accounting record-keeping obligations, after which it is deleted.

Children's data

This website is intended for adults arranging a vehicle purchase, sale or visit, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
