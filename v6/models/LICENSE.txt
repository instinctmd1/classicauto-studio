Classic Auto - website v6 - 3D model, HDRI and font credits
============================================================

Classic Auto is an independent pre-owned car dealer. It is not affiliated with Ferrari. The sports car on the home page
and in the Studio is an illustrative 3D model, never a car for sale.

3D model: models/car.glb
------------------------
  Ferrari 458 Italia by vicent091036 on Sketchfab
  https://sketchfab.com/models/57bf6cc56931426e87494f554df1dab6
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The three.js examples file (three.js r170, examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb), used as published (it is Draco-compressed).
  The maker badge and lettering are removed on the live model at load time (assets/js/carSanitize.js).

Where the model is used:
  - Home page, live: the interactive car in the showroom and at the end of the cloth reveal (assets/js/live3d.js).
  - Home page, baked: hero-frames/ holds frames rendered in Blender from the same model with a dust sheet over it, for
    the cloth reveal and the first-paint poster. The tail badge was painted out of those frames (tools/paint_out_badge.py).
  - Studio (studio.html): the same model, labelled "Illustrative 3D model" in the page.
