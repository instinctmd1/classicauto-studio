Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026. This policy explains what personal data Classic Auto collects through this website and Anita, our chat assistant, why we collect it, and the rights you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

Browsing the stock, using the EMI calculator or looking at the 3D car runs in your browser and does not send us personal data. We collect personal data only when you give it to us: through a test drive, visit, exchange, reserve or sell form, or a conversation with Anita. That can include your name, mobile number, the car you're interested in, your budget, the car you want to sell or exchange, and your preferred date and time of day.

This website does not set cookies and does not use advertising trackers. The intro animation remembers that you have seen it for the length of your browser tab only.

What the site records about visits

To learn which pages and buttons help visitors, the site sends small page-use events to Classic Auto's own server. An event says which page loaded, which car page was opened, which button was pressed (message, visit, book a test drive and so on), which stock filter or Studio view was used, how far the showroom unveil was pulled, and whether a form was sent and by which route. It also carries the site you came from (for example "instagram.com") and any campaign tag in the link you used, taken from the first page of your visit, and whether you use a phone, tablet or computer.

An event never includes your name, mobile number, anything you type into a form or chat, or the full web address with its query. Each event carries a random code made fresh in each browser tab and forgotten when you close the tab. It is not a cookie, it is not stored on your device beyond that tab, and it cannot follow you from one visit to the next. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the site sends no events at all.

Why we collect it

We use it only to respond to your enquiry: to confirm a visit or test drive, follow up a sale, exchange or reserve request, or answer your question. If you tick "Send me new arrivals" we may also message you about new stock. That box is never ticked for you. We do not sell your data.

How it reaches us

Every form on this site sends your message to Classic Auto in one of three ways: directly to our own systems, as a WhatsApp message you send yourself, or as an Instagram direct message you send yourself. When it goes by WhatsApp or Instagram, nothing leaves your device until you tap send.

The feedback form on the Reviews page is private. It goes to the Classic Auto team, is never published, and is not a public review. Name, number, the car you bought and your rating are all optional; the message is required. We contact you about it only if you tick "OK to contact me about this" and gave a number.

Your rights under the DPDP Act

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to ask us to correct or update it, to ask us to erase it once it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it for, to withdraw consent as easily as you gave it, and to nominate someone to exercise these rights for you. To use any of them, contact us as described below and we will act on a verified request within a reasonable time.

Retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to complete the transaction it relates to, plus a reasonable period for follow-up and our legal and accounting records. After that it is deleted.

Children

This website is for adults arranging to buy, sell or visit. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Test drives need a driver aged 18 or over with a valid licence.
