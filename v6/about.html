How it works

  1. See the real price

    Every car is listed with its asking price. Its page also lists the TCS, the RC transfer fee and the insurance status, so you see what you'll pay before you visit.

  2. Plan the money

    Try the EMI estimate, the loan eligibility check and the insurance range on the car's page. They are estimates; the lender and insurer set the real figures.

  3. See it, drive it

    Request a test drive or a visit with a date and a time of day. We confirm by message. Bring your driving licence.

  4. Offer or reserve

    Make an offer or ask us to hold the car. Every request is read by a person; no payment is taken on the website.

  5. Paperwork

    We handle the RC transfer paperwork and tell you the steps for your car.
