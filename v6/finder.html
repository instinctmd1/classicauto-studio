      How we picked these

      Must-haves first. Budget, seats and fuel are hard filters. A car over your budget by more than 10%, with the wrong fuel, or without the seats you need is never in the shortlist. Most listings don't state seats yet, so for 6 or more seats we only count a car whose listing says so, or a model the maker sells only with three rows (sources below).

      Then the rest. Each other answer is worth the points below. A car's fit is the points it wins out of the points possible for the questions you answered; skipped questions don't count. We only score what is on the car's listing. Anything the listing doesn't say (seats, airbag count, some feature lists) is shown as "ask us", never guessed.

        Size words for parking ("a big car") are our own size class (full-size SUVs, MPVs and limousines), not a measurement. "Cheaper per km" for diesel, hybrid, CNG and electric is the usual pattern, not a figure for that car. EMI is indicative: 20% down, 60 months, 11.5% a year assumed; the final EMI depends on the lender.
