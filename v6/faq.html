Can I test drive a car?Buying

Yes. On the car's page choose Test drive, pick a date and a time of day, and we'll confirm by message. You need to be 18 or older with a valid driving licence.

You can also ask for a test drive at your place. That is a request, and we confirm it by message.

Are the prices on the website the real prices?Buying

Yes. Every car is listed with its asking price, and its page lists what you pay on top. Prices can change, so message us for the latest availability.

Can I make an offer?Buying

Yes, with Make an offer on the car's page. We review every offer by hand and message you. Nothing is accepted automatically and nothing is charged.

Can I reserve a car?Buying

Send a reserve request from the car's page and we'll message you to take it forward. No payment is taken on the website.

Are the photos of the actual car?Buying

Each car's photo comes from its listing post on our Instagram, and the car's page links to that post. Ask us for a walk-around video, or come and see the car.

What does "km a year" on a car's page mean?Buying

It is a plain average: the kilometres in the listing divided by the time since the car was registered (per month for cars under a year old). It is worked out fresh every time you open the page.

How is "You save approx." worked out?Buying

Only where the maker publishes a price for the same variant. We take its listed ex-showroom price, show the source and the date we read it, and subtract our asking price. It is approximate.

A new car also needs road tax, registration and insurance on top of its ex-showroom price. We leave the comparison out for cars over three years old, for variants no longer sold new, and once the figure is more than 90 days old.

Do you help with finance?Paying

Yes, easy finance is one of our services. Every car's page has an EMI estimate and a loan eligibility check you can adjust. They are estimates only: the lender decides the amount, rate and approval after checking your documents.

What will I pay on top of the price?Paying

Each car's page lists it under "What you'll pay to drive away": the asking price, 1% TCS on cars over ₹10 lakh, the RC transfer fee and the insurance status.

TCS (tax collected at source) is collected against your PAN and counts as income tax you have already paid. A car registered outside Maharashtra has to be re-registered here if you keep it here, with its own road tax and fees. Ask us for the exact figures before you decide.

Do you buy cars?Selling

Yes. Tell us about your car on the Sell your car page and we'll message you to arrange an inspection.

Can I exchange my car for one of yours?Selling

Yes. Choose "Exchange your car" on any car's page, or use the exchange option on the Sell page. We'll ask for photos by message.

What is Park & Sell?Selling

You park your car with Classic Auto to sell it, and it is shown to buyers alongside our own stock. Message us to talk through how it works for your car.

Who handles the RC transfer?Paperwork

We handle the RC transfer paperwork. How long it takes depends on the RTO, and we'll tell you the steps for your car.

Does the insurance move to me?Paperwork

A valid policy is transferred to the new owner's name along with the RC. Each car's page shows whether its policy is valid and till when, or that a renewal is due.

Is the 3D car in the Studio the car for sale?This website

No. The Studio uses an illustrative 3D model (Ferrari 458 Italia by vicent091036, CC BY 4.0) painted in the listed car's colour. The details beside it, such as price, kilometres and owner, are the real car's. Classic Auto is an independent pre-owned dealer, not affiliated with Ferrari.

What happens to the details I send?This website

They go to the Classic Auto team so we can reply about your enquiry. The site sets no cookies. The privacy policy says what is kept and why.
