    How a Trial Reel works
    1. Publish it as a Trial Reel. Instagram shows it to non-followers only, and you can schedule it.
    2. Wait 24 to 72 hours, then read completion, sends per reach and the share of views from non-followers.
    3. Share it to your followers only if it beats your own median. Otherwise leave it.
    4. Write down the reel type, the hook, the length and the time. After six weeks, keep the three formats with the most sends per reach.

    Marked “test first” here: the reel types with the weakest evidence so far (the Since 1974 series and price games) and anything the plan marks to test first. Delivery-day reels need a customer’s consent first.
