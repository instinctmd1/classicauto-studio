Brand logos used on the home page (Shop by brand) and the stock page brand strip
=========================================================================

Each file is a single-colour (navy-black) vector copy of the maker's mark, cropped to its own outline by tools/make_brand_logos.py.
No file is hotlinked: every logo is served from this folder.

Why they are here: to say which makes Classic Auto sells (nominative use). Every name and logo is a trademark of its owner. Classic Auto is an
independent pre-owned dealer and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any of them. The licence below covers the artwork file only: it gives
no right to the trademark.
