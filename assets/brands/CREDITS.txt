Brand logos used on the home page (Shop by brand) and the stock page brand strip
=========================================================================

Each file is the maker's own logo in its own colours, trimmed to its outline and scaled to one common frame (at most 240 x 150 px) by
tools/make_brand_logos.py, where the maker's mark is a badge plus a wordmark only the badge is kept. No file is hotlinked: every logo is
served from this folder.

Source: the open Car Logos Dataset, https://github.com/filippofilip95/car-logos-dataset (commit e334a4d, 23 Oct 2026), "optimized" PNGs.
Licence: the dataset is MIT licensed (its README: "MIT licensed (logos remain property of respective owners)"). Fetched 15 Nov 2026.
  audi, bmw, ford, honda, hyundai, jaguar, jeep, kia, land-rover, mahindra, maserati, mercedes-benz, mg, mini, skoda, tata, tesla, toyota,
  volkswagen, volvo  <-  logos/optimized/<same name>.png
  maruti-suzuki  <-  logos/optimized/suzuki.png (the Suzuki mark, which Maruti Suzuki India uses)

Why they are here: to say which makes Classic Auto sells (nominative use). Every name and logo is a trademark of its owner. Classic Auto is an
independent pre-owned dealer and is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by any of them. The dataset's licence covers the collection and its
processing code: it gives no right to any trademark.

Replaced on 15 Nov 2026: the one-colour navy SVG copies (Simple Icons, CC0, and three public-domain Wikimedia Commons files for Jaguar,
Land Rover and Mercedes-Benz). They are in this repository's history (commit 035b3f4) if one-colour marks are ever wanted again.
